Hurricanes: Nature's Powerful Storms

Explore the science and impact of one of nature’s most powerful forces.

Understanding Hurricanes

Hurricanes, also known as cyclones or typhoons depending on the region, are massive storm systems that form over warm ocean waters. They bring strong winds, heavy rainfall, storm surges, and flooding to coastal and inland areas.

A hurricane's strength is categorized from 1 to 5 on the Saffir-Simpson scale, with Category 5 being the most intense. These storms can span hundreds of kilometers and cause widespread destruction to homes, power lines, and infrastructure.

The impacts of hurricanes include flooding, wind damage, power outages, and disruption of daily life. Preparing in advance with evacuation plans, emergency supplies, and secure shelters is critical to minimizing harm.

Meteorologists track hurricanes using satellite data, aircraft reconnaissance, and radar systems. Early warnings and expert reports play a key role in informing communities, enabling evacuations, and reducing casualties during hurricane events.
